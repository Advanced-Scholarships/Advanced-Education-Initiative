A New Approach to Educational Philanthropy

The Scholarship System Wasn't Built for Them.

An opportunity to help build a new model for funding advanced education.

Confidential Preview Spring 2027
01

The Opportunity

Millions of adults are going back to school.

Every year, millions of adults decide to return to school to earn a master's degree, MBA, PhD, or advanced credential.

They are experienced. They are working. They have careers. And they are trying to earn the education that could finally move them into a better one.

But the scholarship system largely wasn't built for them.

Platforms such as Fastweb and Bold.org provide thousands of scholarship opportunities, yet graduate and doctoral funding represents only a small portion of the broader scholarship landscape.

For an adult already carrying the responsibilities of life, going back to school can mean choosing between advancing their career and taking on another mountain of debt.

02

A Different Possibility

That shouldn't be the only choice.

Education. Opportunity. Momentum.

A new platform is currently being created to change how advanced education is funded.

03

The Platform

A new model for educational funding.

The platform's name has been decided, but it has not yet been publicly released.

The platform will connect philanthropists, families, companies, and organizations with experienced adults pursuing graduate and doctoral education.

30+

Initial Focus

Adults age 30 and older pursuing graduate, doctoral, and other advanced educational opportunities.

These are people who have already invested years into their careers, their communities, and the people around them—and are now investing in their own next chapter.

04

The Difference

Beyond another scholarship website.

Instead of another endless cycle of applications and essays, the platform is designed around three principles:

01

Direct Access

Create a more direct connection between scholarship opportunities and qualified recipients.

02

Faster Funding

Reduce unnecessary delays between receiving an award and accessing educational support.

03

Greater Accountability

Build transparency into the way scholarship support is managed and documented.

05

The Education Wallet

Scholarship support, managed differently.

Scholarship recipients will have access to a dedicated Education Wallet.

The wallet is designed to allow scholarship funds to be managed for approved educational needs while providing documentation and transparency around how those funds are used.
